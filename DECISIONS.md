| 2026-10-22 | Gamble calibration investigation | The only meaningful >10 pp gap is B3's 50–60% gamble bin (42.6% observed vs 55% midpoint, n = 188). The residual is a policy selection effect (the agent chooses among cells of similar estimated risk using route structure), not a weighting bug. No K, pit density or risk weights were changed | The instruction says fix the cause before tuning; the cause was measured rather than guessed. Changing the policy to close the bin would be agent tuning, which the task forbids |
