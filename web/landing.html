02 / Manor Field Manual

How to Play in Four Steps

Classic Wumpus-World deduction elevated with rotating manor wings, an interactive 4-lesson tutorial, and a fair zero-cheating AI hint whenever you want a second opinion.

STEP 01

Explore & Read Room Senses

Begin at Entrance (0,0). Quiet proves all 4 neighbours safe. Breeze warns of an adjacent Pit; Chill warns of the Ghost; Glimmer signals the Heirloom.

STEP 02

Mark Notes & Cast Charm

Right-click rooms to cycle Safe, Pit, Ghost, or suspect notes. Once you pinpoint the Ghost, press C + direction to cast your 1-use Charm across the manor (+200 score).

STEP 03

Anticipate the 90° Wing Shift

A Rumble warns 2 turns before a 3×3 wing rotates. Hidden hazards and your room notes rotate 90° together — and standing inside a wing holds it steady.

STEP 04

Claim Heirloom & Escape at (0,0)

Step onto the gleaming Heirloom to collect it, navigate back to the golden Entrance door at (0,0) before lantern oil runs out, and press E to leave safely.
