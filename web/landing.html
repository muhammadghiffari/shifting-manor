How to Play Shifting Manor

Simple Wumpus-World logic, supercharged with rotating wings, an in-game 4-lesson tutorial, and a built-in AI coach whenever you want a hint.

1

Explore & Read Senses

Start at Entrance (0,0). Quiet proves all 4 neighbours safe! Breeze warns of an adjacent Pit; Chill warns of the Ghost; Glimmer signals the Heirloom.

2

Mark & Banish

Right-click rooms to place Safe, Pit, Ghost, or ? notes. Have the Ghost cornered? Press C + direction to cast your 1-use Charm straight across the manor (+200 score)!

3

Master the 90° Shift

A Rumble warns 2 moves before a 3×3 wing rotates. Hazards and your notes rotate 90° together — and standing inside a wing holds it still!

4

Grab & Escape at (0,0)

Step onto the gleaming Heirloom to collect it, navigate back to the golden Entrance door at (0,0) before your lantern oil runs out, and press E to escape!
